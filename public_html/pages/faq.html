We Have Answers

Everything you need to know about Community Liberation, our programs, and how to get involved.

Community Liberation is an NGO and charitable organization based in the United Kingdom, India, Ghana and affiliated with the International Liberation Ministry. It aims to support prison inmates, orphans, people in war-prone areas, the needy, and the less privileged.

The mission of Community Liberation is to provide comprehensive support and resources to vulnerable and marginalized populations, including prison inmates, orphans, people in conflict zones, and the underprivileged, to improve their quality of life and foster social inclusion.

Community Liberation offers a variety of services, including:
  • Support for prison inmates through rehabilitation programs, educational resources, and reintegration support.
  • Assistance for orphans, including shelter, education, and emotional support.
  • Aid to people in war-prone areas, such as emergency relief, medical aid, and community rebuilding.
  • Help for the needy through food distribution, financial aid, and access to essential services.

Community Liberation is a subsidiary of the International Liberation Ministry, working in close collaboration to fulfil its mission of providing support and resources to those in need. While affiliated with a ministry, our services are provided to all individuals regardless of their religious beliefs.

There are several ways to get involved:
  • Volunteering your time and skills to support various programs and initiatives.
  • Donating funds or resources to help sustain and expand our services.
  • Partnering with us as a corporate sponsor or community organization.
  • Participating in fundraising events and awareness campaigns.
