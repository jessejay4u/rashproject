Who We Are

Community Liberation

Community Liberation is a dedicated non-governmental and charitable organization based in the United Kingdom, registered under number 16421359. As a subsidiary of the International Liberation Ministry, we are committed to extending the hand of compassion and support to those who need it the most.

Our mission encompasses reaching out to prison inmates, orphans, individuals in war-prone areas, the needy, and the less privileged, offering them hope, support, and the opportunity for a better future.

We operate in the United Kingdom, India, Ghana, and beyond — collaborating with NGOs, government agencies, and community organizations to amplify our efforts.

A Word From

Message from the Founder

"

Dear Friends and Supporters,

It is with great joy and a heart full of gratitude that I welcome you to Community Liberation. As the founder of this remarkable organization, I am deeply committed to our mission of bringing hope, support, and empowerment to some of the most vulnerable members of our global community.

Our journey began with a simple yet profound belief: that every individual, regardless of their circumstances, deserves a chance to live with dignity, purpose, and hope. This belief has driven us to establish comprehensive programs aimed at transforming lives — from supporting prison inmates and caring for orphans to providing aid in war-prone areas and assisting the needy and less privileged.

None of this would be possible without the unwavering support of our donors, volunteers, and partners. Your generosity and dedication enable us to reach more people and make a lasting impact. Together, we are building a global community of compassion, empathy, and mutual support.

Eugene Coal Kwame Gbedemah Founder, Community Liberation

In His Service

Our Mission

Transform lives through comprehensive support and empowerment. We aim to:

  • Support Prison Inmates through rehabilitation and reintegration
  • Care for Orphans in safe, nurturing environments
  • Aid War-Prone Areas with essential relief and recovery support
  • Assist the Needy with food, shelter, healthcare, and training

Our Vision

A world where everyone, regardless of their circumstances, has the opportunity to live a life of dignity, purpose, and hope.

  • Foster a global community supporting the marginalized
  • Create sustainable programs for long-term solutions to poverty
  • Build a global network of compassionate volunteers and partners
