Program 01

Prison Rehabilitation & Reintegration

Community Liberation supports prison inmates through comprehensive programs aimed at rehabilitation and successful reintegration into society. We believe that every person, regardless of their past, has the capacity for transformation and positive contribution.

Educational Workshops Literacy, numeracy and life skills training
Vocational Training Practical skills for employment
Counselling Services Psychological & spiritual guidance
Family Support Assistance for families of inmates

Program 02

Orphan Care & Support

Every child deserves love, safety, and a chance at a bright future. Community Liberation provides a comprehensive care system for orphans — from safe housing and quality education to healthcare and emotional support.

Safe Housing Nurturing environments in orphanages
Education Sponsorship Full schooling from primary to university
Healthcare Regular medical care & mental health support
Mentorship Life skills training & guidance

Program 03

Humanitarian Aid in War-Prone Areas

Conflict tears communities apart, leaving individuals without food, shelter, or medical care. Community Liberation provides immediate relief and long-term recovery support to help rebuild devastated communities.

Emergency Relief Food, water, medicine & shelter kits
Rebuilding Projects Community reconstruction initiatives
Medical Aid Mobile clinics & trauma care
Trauma Counselling Mental health & psychological support

Program 04

Support for the Needy & Less Privileged

Poverty strips people of dignity and opportunity. Community Liberation runs extensive programs to provide food, shelter, healthcare, and skills training to empower the needy towards self-sufficiency.

Food Programs Regular food distribution drives
Shelter Initiatives Housing & temporary shelter support
Healthcare Access Medical care & essential health services
