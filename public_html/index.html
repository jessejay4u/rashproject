About Us

Who We Are

Community Liberation is a dedicated non-governmental and charitable organization based in the United Kingdom (Registration No. 16421359). As a subsidiary of the International Liberation Ministry, we extend the hand of compassion to those who need it most.

  • Registered UK Charity – No. 16421359
  • Subsidiary of International Liberation Ministry
  • Operating in UK, India, Ghana & beyond
  • Non-religious inclusive humanitarian service
"Every individual, regardless of their circumstances, deserves a chance to live with dignity, purpose, and hope."
Eugene Coal Kwame Gbedemah Founder, Community Liberation
