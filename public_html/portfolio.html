Agreement Terms
Terms & Conditions

Payment Terms

All invoices are due on the 1st of each month. A 5-day grace period applies before a late fee of 5% is charged. Payment by bank transfer or mobile money.

Contract Duration

Minimum engagement of 3 months. Thereafter, rolling monthly. 30-day written notice required for cancellation by either party.

Revision & Requests

Each client is entitled to up to 5 change requests per month under the retainer. Additional requests are billed at an agreed hourly rate.

Reporting

Monthly performance reports delivered by the 5th of the following month, covering website analytics, social media metrics, and development updates.

Intellectual Property

All developed assets remain the property of the respective client upon full payment of the monthly retainer for that period.

Rate Review

Rates are subject to a maximum 10% annual review with 30-day advance notice. No mid-contract increases without written agreement.
